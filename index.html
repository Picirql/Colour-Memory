How to play

  1. Keep your eyes on the + in the middle.
  2. Coloured squares will flash very briefly. Try to remember their colours.
  3. One spot will be outlined. Pick the colour that was there on the colour wheel. Move around the wheel to preview, then click / lift your finger.
  4. No rush – accuracy is what counts. Harder rounds (more squares) score more.
