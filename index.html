How to play

  1. Look at the + in the middle. A new round is about to start.
  2. Coloured squares flash for a split second. Remember each square’s colour and where it was.
  3. The squares disappear and one empty box is outlined. That’s where one of the squares was. A colour wheel appears around the screen.
  4. Pick the colour that was in that box. Move your mouse or finger around the wheel – the box fills with that colour. Click, or lift your finger, to choose.
  5. +85
    See how close you were. The box shows the right colour, and the closer your pick, the more points you get.
  • No time limit for picking – take your time.
  • The wheel turns every round, so remember the colour, not where it was on the wheel.
  • More squares = harder rounds = more points.

Your first game starts with 3 practice rounds that don’t count.
